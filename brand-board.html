Cells, not a syringe

05 · Voice

Clinical authority, domestic warmth

Headings end in a double full stop (Essential iron..), which is the one typographic tic the brand owns. Copy is British, second person, and leans on credentials before benefits.

It sounds like

  • "The UK's leading iron deficiency clinic."
  • "The only CQC-regulated provider dedicated exclusively to iron infusions."
  • "We only manage iron deficiency."
  • Plain-English physiology before any sales language.
  • Named clinicians, guideline authorship, publication counts.
  • Client, not patient. Reassured, confident, informed.

It never sounds like

  • Wellness, boosting, detox, or drip-bar language.
  • Exclamation marks or urgency framing.
  • Before/after imagery or body claims.
  • Gendered marketing, despite a women's-health-heavy caseload.
  • Discounting. Price is stated, never promoted.
